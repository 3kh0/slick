# Better Huddles

Slack's normal preview and confirmation screens run first, including microphone, speaker, and camera selection. Only the confirmed `startHuddle` is replaced: Better Huddles calls `rooms.join` and connects directly to Chime. Cancelling the preview does not join the huddle.

Calls open in a separate, movable desktop window by default, with a unified macOS header. Use **Move into Slack** to dock the call, or **Open in separate window** to detach it without reconnecting. **Open huddles as** defaults to a separate window. Floating panels and minimized audio bars inside Slack are opt-in choices in that setting, or in **Layout inside Slack** during a call. These views can be dragged, and floating panels can be resized. **Pop out** always opens a separate desktop window. Closing the separate window leaves the huddle.

## Audio and themes

The call follows the active Slack/Slick theme. **Huddle settings** in the call controls has separate **Microphone** and **Speaker** selectors; changes apply during the call and are remembered for the next one. A disconnected saved device falls back to the system default when joining. Switching microphones preserves mute.

## Video effect

Hover your own tile and click its gear to open the video-effect editor. It sends a GLSL shader as your video, camera on or off (the ✨ button on your own tile toggles it too). Write a Shadertoy-style `mainImage(out vec4 fragColor, in vec2 fragCoord)` with `iResolution`, `iTime`, `iFrame`, and `camera(uv)`, which samples your camera (only shaders that call it open the camera). Output is 640×480 at 24 fps. Templates: pixel plasma (the default), checkerboard tunnel, 8-bit camera, thermal camera, blank.

Standalone huddles (no conversation) and the moment before the window has mounted are left to Slack.

## Privacy

The Chime SDK is only used to talk to Slack's huddle media servers:

- Meeting-event reporting (browser, OS, device, meeting history to Amazon's ingestion service) is off: `media/noTelemetry.ts` drops `EventIngestionUrl`, and `media/chimeMedia.ts` installs the SDK's `NoOpEventReporter`.
- Only the meeting classes are bundled (`media/chimeSdk.ts`); `chimeMeetingOnly` in `scripts/lib/plugin.ts` keeps the SDK's messaging client, SigV4 and `@aws-sdk/*` out of every build. It also gives the SDK's own files a module-local `var global = globalThis` (the SDK expects webpack's Node-style `global`), so nothing is added to Slack's page.
- Logging stays on the page: Chime's messages go to `callLog.ts` (shown, with **Copy logs**, when a join fails or a call drops; Slack tokens and the Chime join token are scrubbed) and warnings to the console (`media/recordingLogger.ts`).
