HaikuWarning bundles the unmodified syllable counts used by Hack Club's
Orpheus bot, pinned to commit aece7f5fcc873b8e71ecccfa5216849fafd959e1:
https://github.com/hackclub/orpheus-bot/blob/aece7f5fcc873b8e71ecccfa5216849fafd959e1/app/lib/haiku_check/syllable_counts.txt

SHA-256: f8f04665147e96cb87d272e464f508b543b80031dfa3b373ccfbdfe2779a3ca6

These pronunciation-derived syllable counts correspond to the Carnegie
Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary (CMUdict). CMUdict is maintained by the
Speech Group, School of Computer Science, Carnegie Mellon University.
Its copyright notice and redistribution terms accompany this file in
CMUDICT-LICENSE.txt.

The data is compiled into the page bundle for offline use. No message
text is sent to a dictionary service and no runtime download is needed.
